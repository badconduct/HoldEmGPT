# House-supplied dossier solutions — spoilers

Story rules version 4. Each complete set has one supported answer, and its false exhibit comes from the responsible boss's house. All four truthful records are necessary, including under the witnessed supplier rule. All five deliveries are required to file the final accusation, which also names the rejected exhibit. This is a consistency guarantee, not a measure of entertainment or natural poker campaign duration.

## Packet 1: The Last Cashout

Victor "The House" Maddox killed Vincent "Velvet" Vale at The Marigold Star using the brass desk ashtray. The gunshot sheet disagrees with both the broad-impact tracing and the brass caught in the jacket. That sheet must be false. The two physical comparisons share only the ashtray. The retained account impressions meet at Maddox, whose house supplied the substituted sheet. The four truthful incident-room checks separately exclude The Gilded Alibi, Casa del Azahar, The Lantern Exchange and Le Salon des Brumes. Only The Marigold Star remains. Each exclusion removes a different competing hotel. The retained records establish a fatal blunt-force head injury from the brass ashtray. The false recovered weapon record was supplied under Victor "The House" Maddox's authority at The Gilded Alibi.

The reserve had been paying Maddox's private debts. The audit would have cost him his house. He used the stranger named on an unsigned invitation as the courier in a cover story.

The weapon claim disagrees with two independent method comparisons. With that one false exhibit accounted for, both account readings and all retained room checks must be true. All four truthful room checks are needed: without any one of them, the hotel it excludes remains a possible crime scene.

The complete dossier went to the investigators in Hollywood. Victor "The House" Maddox was arrested; the forged courier instruction was withdrawn. You were no longer the convenient stranger in their account.
