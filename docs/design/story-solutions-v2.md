# Revised dossier solutions — spoilers

Version 2. Each complete set has one supported answer. Every proper subset leaves alternatives under the one-false rule. This is a logic guarantee, not a measure of entertainment or natural poker campaign duration.

## Packet 1: The Last Cashout

Victor "The House" Maddox killed Vincent "Velvet" Vale at The Marigold Star using the brass desk ashtray. The cashier's sunburst-or-blossom reading and the letter's V. M.-or-L. L. reading meet at Maddox. The piano alcove fixes the Marigold. A broad impact allows ashtray or bottle; polished brass allows ashtray or opener. The ashtray fits both. The revolver sheet is the substituted exhibit: its claimed gunshot fits neither comparison.

The reserve had been paying Maddox's private debts. The audit would have cost him his house. He used the stranger named on an unsigned invitation as the courier in a cover story.

The weapon claim disagrees with two independent method comparisons. With that one false exhibit accounted for, both account readings and the doorway description must be true.

The complete dossier went to the investigators in Hollywood. Victor "The House" Maddox was arrested; the forged courier instruction was withdrawn. You were no longer the convenient stranger in their account.
